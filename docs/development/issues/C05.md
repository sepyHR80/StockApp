GitHub: https://github.com/sepyHR80/StockApp/issues/26

## Goal

Connect chart UI to daily candles with date validation and visible source/freshness/coverage.

## Scope

In scope: Connect chart UI to daily candles with date validation and visible source/freshness/coverage.

Out of scope: Minute charts or mixing synthetic and real series.
